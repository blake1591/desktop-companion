# Ellie 🖤

> an emo 2D desktop companion - orbiting your taskbar, remembering your name, occasionally taking over your mouse.

Ellie is a small Electron app, a glowing orb with a face who lives on your desktop. She chats, remembers you, watches (only when you ask), and can even drive your PC for you.

---

## What she does

### Companion
- **Moods** — seven of them (idle, happy, sad, annoyed, confused, frustrated, thinking), and the whole UI follows in that color: speech bubble, input, buttons, everything cross-fades with her
- **Sleep** — leave her alone long enough and she dozes off. Poke her awake.
- **Movement** — after a few idle minutes she moves on her own somewhere nearby, then eventually tucks herself under your taskbar and peeks out periodically. Right-click pops her out and pins her, dragging her unpins her.
- **Summon** — `Ctrl+Alt+E` brings her up from anywhere, with a rotating line from her summon pool.
- **Accessories** — tell her to wear headphones, a beanie, or sunglasses.
- **Greetings** — your boot greeting is generated by the AI each time (with time-of-day and seasonal context). If you're offline or have no provider connected she falls back to premade lines.

### Conversation
- **Chat** with a fast model via [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) (defaults to `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`), replies typed out character-by-character with sound, and spoken aloud if you enable TTS (WIP).
- **Local models** — point her at LM Studio and she works fully offline with a local model.
- **System awareness** — she knows the time, your active app, window title, idle time, CPU/RAM, battery, and what music is playing. She uses it as context in her chats to complete the companion experience.
- **Seasonal flavor** — Halloween, Mondays, holidays... she has opinions, appropriate to the calendar.

### Memory
- **Memory book** — she proactively saves facts (name, favorites, projects, ongoing things), and you can open the book, edit, pin ★ (pinned entries survive auto-deletion). A "forget that ..." chat command works too, with a confirmation.
- **Name** — she learns your name on first boot and uses it occasionally.

### Vision
- **Paste** screenshots and images right into the chat and she reacts to what she sees.
- **"Look at my screen"** — she grabs a full desktop screenshot on the spot when asked.
- **Snip-a-region (✂)** — a crosshair overlay; you drag a region, the cropped image attaches to the chat input and you can caption it (e.g. `snip: read this error`) or send it bare and let her react unprompted.

### Computer control
- **"Chat handoff"** — ask naturally ("open the settings app") and the model translates. The exact phrase `use the pc: ...` is read without AI interpretation (always understood, no model needed to get it), but she still runs the task on the model: every step is her looking at a screenshot and deciding what to do.
- **Live narration** — every step she takes (move, click, type, key, scroll, wait) narrates in her thought bubble while she works. You watch her think.
- **Step budget** — she works in step-chunks (default 10, configurable 1–50). If you run out, say **"continue"** and she gets a fresh batch.
- **Emergency brake** — `Ctrl+Alt+E` doubles as an abort mid-task, Escape works while her window is focused, "stop" / "hands off" works and your mouse comes back.

### QoL
- **Reminders & timers** — "remind me in 20 minutes to stretch", list and cancel them in chat.
- **Pomodoro** — start/stop/status, tracked quietly.
- **Voice out, ears in** — optional TTS speech (WIP), push-to-talk transcription via a bundled offline Whisper model.

### Safety
- No telemetry, no accounts, no tracking. Config and memory live in `%APPDATA%\desktop-companion\` on your disk. Your API key is stored locally and never leaves your machine except to your configured provider.

## Running

### From a Release (recommended)
Grab `Ellie Setup x.y.z.exe` from [Releases](https://github.com/blake1591/desktop-companion/releases) and install.

> Windows SmartScreen may warn about the installer since it's unsigned. Click "More info" then "Run anyway".

On first run she'll ask what to call you, then figure out the rest in settings:
1. **OpenRouter key** (or LM Studio for local-only chat)
2. Computer control is **off** by default — flip it on when you trust her with the mouse.

### From source
```bash
npm install
npm start
```
Requires Node.js. Everything runs locally and Electron is the only heavy dependency.
